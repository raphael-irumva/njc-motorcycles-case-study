NJC Motorcycles — Website Development

Project Overview

NJC Motorcycles is a motorcycle business based in South Africa, offering new and second-hand motorcycles alongside related services.

This project involved the collaborative development and improvement of the company's online presence through a responsive business website with e-commerce functionality.

Live Website: https://njcmotorcycle.com/

Key Features

1. Business Presentation: Structured pages introducing the company, its activities and services.
2. Motorcycle Catalogue: Online presentation of motorcycle products.
3. E-commerce: Online shopping functionality.
4. Service Information: Dedicated pages presenting business services.
5. Customer Engagement: Contact and enquiry functionality.
6. Responsive Interface: Website layout adapted for desktop and mobile devices.

My Contributions

I participated in this project as a member of the website development team, with responsibilities focused on:

- Website Page Development: Creating, organizing and customizing website pages to improve content presentation and navigation.
- SEO Optimization: Improving page content and search engine optimization to strengthen the website's online visibility.
- Responsive Design: Improving the website's presentation and usability across different screen sizes and mobile devices.
- Website Maintenance: Performing website updates, improvements and maintenance to support its functionality and overall quality.
